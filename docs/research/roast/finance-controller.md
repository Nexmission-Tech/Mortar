# Mortar: A Finance Controller’s Roast

## First Impression

I can see who should chase the booking, which is already better than three
departments assuring me it is progressing. But replacing 50 bookings with 21
expected signatures does not give me a cash-flow forecast. Before I back your
pilot, reconcile the population, show me the evidence behind a signature and
tell me how wrong those probabilities have been.

## The Roast

Rank means influence on my pilot decision. **Already Raised** identifies a gap
in the operations review or the sales director’s roast; it is not another
backlog item. Figures below are reproduced from the seeded demonstration as at
**18 September 2026**, with the Manager’s full scope, not Chin Hin results.

The sponsor says: “Sales does not control bank credit decisions” and “Finance
views a booking that does not convert as not being a sale, while it still
consumes inventory, marketing resources, and other costs.” Its warning is
“forecasting from a number that is known to be unreliable”. I want that
uncertainty exposed without turning the launch queue into another approval gate.

## Report

**Pilot Verdict:** I would back a bounded, one-project pilot proposal today and
authorise its synthetic workflow rehearsal. I would not authorise real buyer
data in this public demo or replace the cash-flow forecast with its signing
outlook. The named follow-up owner, inspectable case rules and visible exit
register earn the trial; a financial benefit has not yet been established.

The three questions Mortar answers worst are **how expected signatures become
dated cash**, **how accurate its probabilities are on real bookings**, and
**what audited quarterly improvement the intervention causes**. All three
limitations were already raised; none needs another feature proposal here.

**Verification Limits:** I inspected the supplied screens, pitch, deck, sponsor
wording, product descriptions, prior reviews and current rules. Read-only
calculations reproduced the populations, values and synthetic back-test above;
they are not a live-server or browser audit. I could not verify real execution
evidence, production access or data-processing arrangements, cash
reconciliation, forecast calibration on Chin Hin data, or measured pilot uplift.
The requested root-level project overview was absent, so I read its relocated
copy. The full project check stopped because the TypeScript compiler was
unavailable; the repository formatter was also unavailable. Focused checks
passed for the quoted figures, document structure and exact pitch quotations.

**Next Action:** Spend two minutes rehearsing Rank 2’s reconciliation aloud,
including the 25 exited and nine older bookings.
